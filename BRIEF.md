# Little Train — approved direction

A fun, cute, Montessori-inspired toy train world for three-year-olds. Child-directed choices,
calm repetition, tangible cause/effect. No reading-dependent progress, punishment, timers,
scores, ads, accounts or purchases. Montessori-inspired is not a certification claim.

The user approved the round-one screen concepts on September 20 and asked to build the full
flow. Menu, loading, destination selection, meadow driving, station visits and orchard cargo.
One camera button toggles overview/follow/cab; no separate camera-selection screen.

Take the fire truck gameplay audit forward: simple motor controls must preserve decisions.
Meadow objects respond to touch, station friends have child-chosen seating, and orchard trees
can be harvested in any order. Carry the result in the train, then show an illustrated success modal with Back to menu and optional deliberate replay.
Never require a different place or force immediate replay.

Secondary goal: optional English words and phrases grounded in play. Model, leave space,
allow replay. Parent-selectable phrase lengths and contextual social language. No recognition,
recording, speech scoring, forced repeats, forced apologies or speech-dependent game gates.
The game supports shared play, not individualized speech therapy. See docs/LANGUAGE-PLAY.md.

Landscape iPhone/iPad PWA, warm offline launch including speech, non-interrupting updates,
fixed 60 Hz simulation and target 60 FPS. Test on named physical devices before claiming
mobile performance or preschool acceptance. Current route is a single guided loop.
