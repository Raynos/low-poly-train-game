# Language through shared play

Fun and child-directed exploration come first. The optional English layer supports
shared play, not therapy, assessment, or a claim of improved outcomes. No microphone,
recording, recognition, pronunciation scores, diagnosis or speech-dependent progress.
No personal child information is stored.

## Sources reviewed September 20, 2026

- [ASHA: Late Language Emergence](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/late-language-emergence/): caregiver involvement and individual assessment matter; treatment depends on the whole communication profile.
- [ASHA: Spoken Language Disorders](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/spoken-language-disorders/): play-based language opportunities and expanding/recasting utterances are described approaches. This game applies broad ideas, not a treatment protocol.
- [AAP: Language Delays in Toddlers](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/toddler/Pages/Language-Delay.aspx): encourage communication through shared play, reading and conversation; concerns warrant evaluation, potentially including hearing and speech-language assessment.

## Implemented

- Contextual models: train, go, stop, tree, water, sheep, bridge, station, apple, hello, in, full.
- Parent chooses single words, short phrases or little sentences. Never an automatic ability estimate.
- Five-second gap between automatic cues; product pacing, not a clinical dose. Explicit replay remains available.
- Gestures, sounds, words and silence are welcome. Speaking never unlocks content.
- Optional word book: please, help, thanks, more, turns and sorry, with caregiver examples.
  Model sorry alongside helping repair a small pretend mishap. Never require an apology.
- Grown-up settings encourage following interest, expanding by a word, waiting for a response,
  and trying the same language afterward with toys or snacks.
- 60 locally generated neural English clips (user-selected Kokoro af_nicole, American English, speed 0.92),
  bundled as MP3s and covered by the offline cache. See docs/design/remaster/VOICE.md for reproduction.
- Three illustrated contextual word buttons remain available in every destination and activity phase.
- Audio requires a gesture, and spoken words can be muted separately. Headless tests use mute=1.
