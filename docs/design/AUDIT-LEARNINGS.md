# Applying the fire truck review

Read the Codex task “Review toddler gameplay depth” and sibling
docs/evidence/gameplay-review/review.md on September 20, 2026.

Low motor demands must not remove decisions. More buttons or longer holds do not create
agency, and technical completion does not establish child engagement.

Applied here:
- Different interactions: meadow discoveries, choosing passengers/seats, harvesting chosen trees.
- A drive hold cannot complete a station/orchard task. Occupied seats preserve selection for retry.
- Loaded passengers/apples travel in the train before completion.
- Where next returns to destination selection; the same place remains freely available.
- Optional meadow responses and language cues follow what the child explores.
- One camera button cycles all views without resetting the journey.

Limitation: driving remains guided on one loop, without rail switches or alternate routes.
Child observation is still needed to assess engagement, not just task completion.
