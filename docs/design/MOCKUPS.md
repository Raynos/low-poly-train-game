# Original mockup brief — completed

This brief led to the approved round-one set in ROUND-1.md.

Original plan: compare three coherent directions at landscape phone and iPad sizes:

1. Toy railway overview: whole loop visible, child sees the direct effect of hold/release.
2. Follow the locomotive: closer train silhouette and recognizable destinations, restrained camera.
3. Driver's cab: one large physical go/stop control, forward track view, minimal dashboard.

Use chunky teal/ochre train shapes, calm natural colors and concrete familiar scenery as a
starting hypothesis. The initial blockout was replaced after full-set approval.

For the preferred direction, show loading, home, starting, driving, stopping at a platform,
and choosing to repeat. Retain the same train proportions and control positions throughout.
Compare phone-size legibility, whether a child can infer the primary action, reachability,
visual clutter and achievable geometry budgets. Confirm control scheme through supervised play.

Keep prompts, alternatives and decisions here; store concepts under assets/design/ outside
public/. Label all generated images as concepts. Capture implemented gameplay separately in
docs/evidence/. Resolve camera/art direction with the user before polishing the world.
